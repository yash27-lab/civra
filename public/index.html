THE PROBLEM

Permit work is hard to follow.

Small business owners must search many city pages, read long forms, find old files, and remember due dates. One missed step can stop the business from working.

OUR ANSWER

Civra keeps every step clear.

Civra checks its supported NYC permit page, reviews permit documents against its requirement set, and shows the evidence behind each result. You decide what to do next.
